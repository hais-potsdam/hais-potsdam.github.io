# HAIS design guide

## Reference files

- `logo.png`: HAIS logo (sitting "mr. net" figure silhouette) in white on olive (`#49513d`) background

- `website-reference.png`: Screenshot of https://hpi-ai-safety.de for visual reference

## Identity

- **HAIS** means **HPI AI Safety Initiative**. We are an independent student initiative at HPI in Potsdam, open to HPI students and people from Potsdam. We organize AI safety events and a reading circle and help students find ways to make AI safer.

Do not imply HPI sponsors or endorses us; do not use HPI logo.

## Visual style

- **Look:** Calm, editorial, academic and welcoming. Generous whitespace, left-aligned text, simple grids and thin rules. Avoid glossy gradients, neon tech motifs and alarmist imagery.
- **Colors:** Paper `#fafaf6`, charcoal text `#252923`, olive accent `#49513d`; supporting colors: muted `#62685e`, pale sage `#edf0e8`, divider `#cdd2c7`.
- **Type:** Noto Serif (or Georgia) for sentence-case headlines and body; Noto Sans for small labels and calls to action. Keep text legible at the final size.
- **Logo and photos:** Keep the square olive-and-white logo intact. Prefer approved, real, candid photos of students and events. If none are supplied, use a text-led layout; do not invent people or events with AI imagery.

## Communication

- **Voice:** Clear, warm, direct English; serious about AI risk without hype. Invite participation. Example tone: “We are a group of students at HPI working to make AI safer.”
- **Event graphics:** Prioritize the title, verified date/time, location and one call to action. Never invent speakers, details, links or affiliations; use a placeholder if information is missing.

## Prompt starter

- “Create a [format and size] for HAIS about [topic]. Use the supplied logo and screenshot, follow this guide, and include only this verified copy: [copy]. Use [approved photo / no photo]. Keep it readable at final size.”
